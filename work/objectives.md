## Primary Objectives
- [ ] Discuss HPWAN deployment on topologies for public networks and service scenarios based on the HP-WAN framework.
- [ ] Review controllers and orchestrators for integrated HPWAN connection management (ESNET)
- [ ] Test integration and simulation of the HP-WAN functions, such as rate negotiation, admission control, traffic scheduling, and resource reservations with distributed signalling (IETF RSVP-TE).
- [ ] Perform congestion control algorithm optimizations (e.g., DCQCN) to simulate traffic transmission with negotiated rates and compare results with the HPWAN approach.
- [ ] Ensure end-to-end monitoring of HPWAN services by evaluating IETF tooling, determining whether it is suitable, and identifying gaps where improvements are needed for cooperation between HPWAN domains.
- [ ] Define high-level HPWAN service model to request HPWAN services across transports domain.
